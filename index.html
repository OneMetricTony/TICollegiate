About Us

A private high school built around the student

Toronto International Collegiate is a private high school inspected by the Ontario Ministry of Education (BSID 669195). We offer Ontario credit courses leading to the Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD), along with tutoring, language-test preparation, workshops, and support services including homestay and visa assistance.
